How to Play

Explore all four squares and discover their unique interactions.

  • 🔢 Square 1: Hover to generate a random number (0–99). Double-click to change its background colour.
  • 🎨 Square 2: Hover to switch between green and red backgrounds.
  • 🌈 Square 3: Hover to generate a random RGB background colour.
  • 🪄 Square 4: Click to change the background colours of the first three squares.

Move your cursor away from a square to reset its colour or number where applicable.
